# Results stars (timed / daily)

Scoring uses combo chains, bombs, and rainbows (see `release()` in `src/sim.ts`).

| Stars | Score (approx.) | Notes |
|------:|----------------:|-------|
| 1 | under 12,000 | Survived the round |
| 2 | 12,000–27,999 | Strong greedy bot / good casual run |
| 3 | 28,000+ | Combos, bombs, and rainbows |

Greedy timed bot on seed `feel` (7×11) lands about **10–22k** without overtime; human play with specials can reach 3★.

Scoring per clear: `4×(i+1)` per piece, bomb +45, rainbow +120, combo up to ×5.
