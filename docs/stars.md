# Results stars (timed / daily)

Scoring uses combo chains, bombs, and rainbows (see `release()` in `src/sim.ts`).

| Stars | Score (approx.) | Notes |
|------:|----------------:|-------|
| 1 | any finish | Completed the round |
| 2 | 20,000+ | Strong run |
| 3 | 38,000+ | Skilled long-chain play |

Greedy longest-chain touch QA ~**26–33k**; skilled long-chain ~**39k**.

Scoring per clear: `4×(i+1)` per piece in the chain, **+25% chain points** at 7+ links, bomb +45, rainbow +120, combo up to ×5.
